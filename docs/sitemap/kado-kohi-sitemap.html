How to read this map

Think of the website like a store with rooms. The homepage is the entrance. Each colored box is a room (page). Boxes under it are what you can do inside that room.

Start / home / portal
Main section
Page or screen

URLs in small text (like /menu) are the web addresses. Guests usually click buttons and menus — they do not need to type these.

Who uses the website

Five kinds of people. Each sees different doors.

Guest

Anyone browsing. Can order via QR or website cart without a full account for some flows; can browse everything public.

Customer

Logged-in guest. Sees My Account: orders, loyalty stamps, vouchers, booth bookings, profile.

Barista

In-store coffee team. Runs the order board, POS, stock toggles, loyalty stamps, customer kiosk screen.

Staff

Fulfillment team. Handles merch orders, booth/event bookings, and an all-orders view.

Admin

Owner / manager. Full control: menu, branches, users, payments settings, homepage content, events, and more.

Important for owners: Customers sign in at the normal website login. Admin, barista, and staff use a separate, hidden login page (Management Portal) that is not listed in the public menu.

2. How people place orders (journeys)

Orders can start in different ways. All paid online drinks/merch go through the same kitchen and payment rules.

Table QR (dine-in)
Scan QR on the table /order/qr/…
Pick drinks / pastries / cookies Pay (GCash or PayMongo) Kitchen prepares · bring to table
Takeout QR
Scan takeout QR /order/takeout
Choose branch if needed Order · pay · pick up
Website cart
Add items from Menu / Merch / Pastries
Open cart drawer Place order · pay on checkout page Track status (guest page or My Account)
Walk-in POS
Barista or Admin POS screen
Cash or GCash at counter Order appears on barista board
