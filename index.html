Kado Coffee — Ceremonial Grade Matcha & Specialty Coffee

Kado Coffee (Kado Kohi) is a Japanese-inspired specialty cafe serving ceremonial grade matcha and specialty coffee across Metro Manila — Marikina, Greenhills, and expanding. Guests searching for matcha near me, specialty coffee, or a cozy tambayan find us rated 4.9 stars on Google with warm barista service.

Our menu highlights ceremonial grade matcha and hojicha drinks made with quality ingredients. Try the Matcha Oat Latte, Dirty Matcha Oat Latte, Matcha Strawberry Oat Latte, Hojicha Oat Latte, or Salted Cream Hojicha Oat Latte — popular choices for oat latte lovers across Metro Manila. Signature espresso drinks include the KADO Latte with torched muscovado, Ube Shio Karamel Latte, and Yuzu AmeriKado.

Classic lattes such as Spanish Latte, Moka Latte, and Karamel Latte are available hot or iced with regular milk or oat milk. Refreshing Yuzu Lime Soda and other yuzu sodas round out the menu for guests who want something bright and citrus-forward after coffee or matcha.

Visit us for dine-in, table QR ordering, takeout, or online GCash checkout during open hours. We host tambayan events and community nights — see our events calendar. Book our mobile coffee booth for weddings, birthdays, and corporate functions across Metro Manila.

Browse the full coffee and matcha menu, find branch locations, learn about Kado Kohi, shop merch, or contact us for questions. Follow @kadocoffeeph on Instagram and KadoKohi on Facebook for menu updates, seasonal drinks, and booth availability.
